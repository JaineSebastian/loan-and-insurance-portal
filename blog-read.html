Understanding Home Loan Interest Rates

Home Loan • January 2025 • By Livelong Shield

Buying a home is one of the biggest financial decisions in life. Understanding how home loan interest rates work can save you lakhs over the loan tenure.

Banks evaluate multiple factors such as your credit score, income stability, employment type and existing liabilities before deciding your rate of interest.

“A small difference in interest rate can result in massive savings over a 20–30 year tenure.”

Maintaining a strong CIBIL score, choosing the right tenure and negotiating with lenders through professional advisors can significantly reduce your EMI burden.
